## 2. Why it exists

We build keyboards for hundreds of millions of people, and our goal has always been to take the friction out of expression. In practice, we shipped one improvement after another to make the keyboard faster and smarter, but when we stepped back and looked at the whole process from a thought to a sent message, typing turned out to be only one of the bottlenecks.

Thought → organize → choose words → type → format → self-edit → send

Every step of that process is paid for in the detail and nuance that carry your actual meaning. A rough thought has to be rebuilt into proper written structure before any of it can be typed. You stall on wording, then take the word you can type fast over the one you wanted. You cut detail because every extra sentence is more typing. You stop at good enough rather than right. When you are moving or your hands are busy, you put it off, and some of it never gets sent.

The distance between what you meant and what you sent is the **Expression Gap**.

Each attempt to close it traded one cost for another. Faster keyboards and swipe typing made the typing quicker and left the rest of the process in place. Dictation removed the typing and handed back raw speech, so the cleanup you skipped before now waits for you afterward. AI writing tools took the writing, and handed back a draft the reader can tell is AI. You have to edit it until it sounds like you.

Voiskey makes no trade. It removes the process. You speak the thought as it naturally comes, and Voiskey does the sorting, the reshaping and the writing. No detail cut for speed, no settling on words, no rewriting when a second thought arrives, no worrying whether the tone is right. What lands is what you would have written if typing had cost nothing.

**Voice is the key.**

## 3. Who it is for

Voiskey is for anyone whose thinking outruns their typing.

Users are grouped by the kind of Expression Gap they experience, not by job title. One person can have more than one, and which one dominates changes with the moment.

| Gap | What the user needs | Typical of |
|---|---|---|
| **Speed** | To write as fast as they think. When they can't, they send less than they meant: the shorter reply, the dropped detail, the thing they meant to add | PMs, consultants, founders, sales leads, AI power users writing prompts |
| **Fidelity** | To still sound like themselves. When a tool cleans it up, the tone, the style and the phrasing tend to go with it | Creators, newsletter writers, podcasters, anyone whose audience knows their voice |
| **Availability** | To write when typing isn't possible or comfortable. Hands are full, they're moving, there's no keyboard, or the room is too quiet to speak up in, so the message waits | New parents, commuters, drivers, people in open offices and shared spaces |
| **Load** | To write without the act of typing being the barrier. When typing itself is the hard part, whether from attention, stamina or motor effort, the thought stalls before it lands | ADHD and other neurodivergent users, people with motor or dexterity limits, long-form thinkers, people who draft by talking |

## 4. What it does

| Feature | Mechanism | Value |
|---|---|---|
| **Dictation** | Converts speech to text, resolving disfluency (fillers, false starts, repetition, mid-sentence correction) and applying the formatting the output calls for | Text arrives finished and still sounds like the user. The polish does not flatten how they talk. No editing pass between speaking and sending |
| **Context Awareness** | Detects the active application and adapts tone, format and register to it | Output matches what the destination expects in register, structure, length and convention, without the user adjusting anything |
| **Personal Dictionary** | Stores and syncs user-specific names, companies, terms and phrases, learning from corrections | The user's own vocabulary stops causing errors. A correction is made once |
| **Whisper Input** | Recognizes low-volume speech, including against background noise | Voice stays available in the places where speaking aloud is not an option: open offices, shared rooms, transit, late at night |
| **Privacy Mode** | On by default. Speech is processed securely and privately. Nothing is retained and no user data is used for training | Nothing a user says is stored or used to train a model |
| **Translation** | Translates dictated speech into a target language at output | The user works in their own language and the recipient reads native text |
| **Ask AI** | Accepts spoken instructions and questions, writing results into the active field or returning an answer in a window, including about selected text. Powered by Google Gemini | AI is reachable without switching windows or rebuilding context. A spoken question or instruction is enough |
| **Sync** | Carries settings, Personal Dictionary and subscription state between a user's devices | Personalization follows the user rather than the device |
| **Universal Input** | Available in every application and text field. A system keyboard on mobile, system-wide input on desktop. The mobile keyboard also supports standard typing | Voiskey works wherever the user already writes, with nothing to copy between apps and nothing to switch to when speaking is not an option |

## 5. What Voiskey is not

**Not an AI.** Voiskey uses AI. It is not one. It is a product built on models, not a model itself.

**Not a voice agent.** Voiskey does not execute tasks, invoke files or functions, or run multi-step operations on the user's behalf. Dictation never rewrites the user, and Ask AI generates only when asked. Voiskey acts on what is said, never on the user's behalf.

**Not a single-purpose app.** Voiskey serves note-taking, meeting capture and translation without claiming any of them as its category. Claiming one narrows what it is understood to do.

The team has decades of experience building input methods at scale, with products reaching 100M+ DAU and 1B+ cumulative downloads. That experience in keyboard interaction, dictionaries, language switching and high-frequency input habits is why the team rebuilds the keyboard rather than adds voice to one.
