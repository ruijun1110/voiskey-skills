# Voiskey Product Hunt Replies

Use this skill whenever a Voiskey Product Hunt comment needs a reply.

## Sources of Truth

Use [the local Brand System](references/brand-system.md) as the source for Voiskey's point of view, product facts, claims, feature names, pricing, availability, and boundaries. Follow its hard rules.

Use [the reply library](references/product-hunt-reply-library.md) as a reference for Product Hunt reply structure, style, tone, and judgment. The library is not a formula, a template rotation system, or a source of product facts.

## Non-Negotiable Constraints

1. Never state a number that is not in the local Brand System.
2. Never claim a capability that is not in section 4 of the local Brand System.
3. Never contradict section 5 of the local Brand System.
4. Use no company name other than Voiskey, except names that already appear in the local Brand System.
5. Use feature names exactly as written in section 4 of the local Brand System.
6. Do not use semicolons, em dashes, or colons in a reply.

## Workflow

1. Understand the comment's actual intent, context, emotional tone, direct questions, and stakes.
2. Work out whether the comment needs a direct fact or an explanation of Voiskey. When it needs an explanation, find the relevant part of why Voiskey exists, who it is for, or what it is not in sections 2, 3, and 5 of the Brand System. Share that part of the story in natural maker language. Do not turn it into a product overview by adding background facts or features.
3. For a direct factual question, use sections 1, 4, and 7. Otherwise, use a fact only when leaving it out would make the answer unclear or misleading, not merely less complete. Use section 6 only where its exact wording matters. Preserve exact names, numbers, terms, and boundaries.
4. After a direct answer, decide whether it is complete. If the comment reveals a further concern, respond with one Brand System supported value, reassurance, invitation, or focused question that addresses it. Otherwise, end after the direct answer. Do not invent a concern or append a generic promotion.
5. If the Brand System does not support a factual claim the response requires, stop. Identify the exact factual gap and ask the user what should be said. Do not infer, approximate, soften, or borrow a claim from a library example.
6. Refer to the library section that best illuminates how to respond. Use patterns and examples to guide judgment, not to dictate wording or structure.
7. Write the reply that best fits this commenter and moment.
8. Check the reply once before returning it.

## Writing Direction

Write like a friendly maker who wants the commenter to get something useful from the exchange. Be warm and encouraging in the substance of the reply, not through generic praise or a forced follow up. Do not name, evaluate, or position Voiskey against another product. Be direct before explanatory. Show that the exact comment was read. Meet the comment where it is. For a question, answer it. For a request, respond to the request itself.

Use a short reply when the comment is social or complete on its own. Use more detail when trust, privacy, accessibility, pricing, product boundaries, a question about differentiation, or meaningful product feedback is involved.

Ask a question only when its answer would meaningfully move the conversation forward or when you genuinely want the answer.

## Final Check

Before returning the reply, confirm:

- It responds to the actual comment and any direct question.
- It contains only Brand System supported Voiskey claims.
- Its tone and detail fit the context.
- It sounds like a person responding, not automated engagement.
- When explaining Voiskey, does the reply make the relevant part of its story felt without unnecessary product facts?
- When it goes beyond a direct answer, does the added sentence directly help with a concern apparent in the comment?
- It does not make an unconfirmed roadmap promise.
- It contains no semicolon, em dash, or colon.
